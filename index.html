Privacy Policy — THE BILL: Factory Roguelite

Last updated: 2026-11-22

THE BILL: Factory Roguelite ("the Game") is developed and published by ZHAO QINGLIN. This policy explains what data the Game does and does not handle.

1. We do not collect personal data.

The Game does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no registration, no email collection, and no advertising or analytics SDKs in the build.

2. No telemetry.

The Game does not send gameplay statistics, device identifiers, or crash reports to us or to any third party. All gameplay data (progress, settings, local scores) is stored on your own device and never leaves it.

3. Steam features are handled by Valve.

Achievements, cloud saves, and playtime are provided by the Steam client through the Steamworks API. That data is processed by Valve Corporation under the Steam Subscriber Agreement and Valve's Privacy Policy (https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/). We only receive aggregate, non-identifying achievement statistics through the Steamworks partner dashboard.

4. Children.

The Game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Your rights.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. If you believe we hold data about you, contact us and we will confirm and delete it.

6. Changes.

If a future update introduces any data collection, this policy will be updated before that update ships, and the change will be noted in the patch notes.

本游戏由 赵青林 开发与发行。本政策说明本游戏处理与不处理哪些数据。

1. 我们不收集个人信息。

本游戏不收集、不传输、不存储、不出售任何个人信息。没有账号系统、没有注册、不收集邮箱，构建中不含任何广告或数据分析 SDK。

2. 无遥测。

本游戏不向我们或任何第三方向外发送游戏统计数据、设备标识或崩溃报告。所有游戏数据（进度、设置、本地分数）仅保存在你自己的设备上，不会离开本机。

3. Steam 功能由 Valve 处理。

成就、云存档、游戏时长由 Steam 客户端通过 Steamworks API 提供。这些数据由 Valve Corporation 依据《Steam 订户协议》与其隐私政策处理（https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/）。我们仅通过 Steamworks 合作伙伴后台看到聚合的、不含身份信息的成就统计。

4. 儿童。

本游戏不收集任何人的信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

5. 你的权利。

因为我们不持有你的个人数据，所以没有可导出或删除的内容。若你认为我们持有你的数据，请联系我们，我们会核实并删除。

6. 政策变更。

若未来更新引入任何数据收集，我们会在该更新上线前更新本政策，并在更新说明中注明。
